02 · Why only an iQOO 15

A shop audit needs sensors, not servers.

Chaukas is not an app that happens to run on a phone. Every step of the workflow is anchored to a specific piece of iQOO 15 hardware, most of which ordinary phones, iPhones and laptops simply do not have.

  • 1
    50MP Ultra-wide · 119°

    1/2.76" sensor. The whole 1.5 m counter in one frame, 30 packets, no stitching.

  • 2
    50MP 3× Periscope telemacro

    Sony IMX882, 1/1.95". Reads faint dot-matrix expiry dates from 25 cm, without the phone's shadow.

  • 3
    50MP Sony IMX921 main

    1/1.56" sensor with iQOO's own OIS for sharp bill and challan photos in dim shop light.

  • 4
    Color Spectrum sensor + triple ambient light sensors

    Detects 50 Hz tube-light flicker and glare on foil packs before the shot.

09:00 The distributor arrives

Tap. Stamp. Count.

Rajesh from the Nestlé distributor walks in with cartons. Ramesh doesn't type anything.

  1. NFC
    1-tap check-in

    Rajesh taps his ID card on the phone and the vendor ledger opens in <100 ms.

  2. NavIC
    Proof-of-delivery stamp

    India's own satellite system geo-tags the delivery; a SHA-256 hash makes it tamper-proof.

  3. Spectrum
    Kill the flicker

    Color Spectrum sensor locks 50 Hz anti-banding so foil packets don't blind the camera.

  4. UW 119°
    One wide photo

    Ultra-wide captures the entire counter in a single frame.

  5. YOLO11n
    Counted on the NPU

    INT8 detector on the Hexagon NPU counts 20 packets in ~22 ms.

11:30 Ice-cream arrives

The phone talks to the freezer.

When dairy or ice-cream is logged, the iQOO 15's top-frame IR blaster fires a 38 kHz remote command and pushes the old deep-freezer into super-freeze. No smart plug, no Wi-Fi, the phone is the remote.

16:00 Rush hour

Stop. That packet expired.

A customer picks an old packet from the back of the shelf. When it's scanned, the batch check fails and the phone physically rumbles for 500 ms on its dual-axis (X + Z) vibration motor while the screen turns red. In an 80 dB bazaar, a vibration in the hand beats a beep nobody hears.

Dual-axis X+Z motor3× telemacro date readSQLite batch registry
Zero loss: the app also reminds Ramesh to return near-expiry stock to the distributor inside the 30-day window for full credit.

19:30 Flour on his hands

Just ask. In Hindi.

Ramesh“Rajesh vendor ne kitna kam maal diya hai?”
Phone · loud“Rajesh vendor se 4 packet Maggi kam aaye the, kul ₹56 kaatne baaki hain.”

Whisper speech-to-text and the Laya intent model run on the NPU, turn the question into a fixed SQL query, and the answer is read out on the stereo speakers, a built-in shop soundbox. The language model only phrases facts from the database; it never invents numbers.

12 · Mapped to the finale criteria

Built for how the finale is judged.

Weights are our estimate from the published format (phone-first Red Light phase, Office Kit Green Light phase, live demo).

Phone-first execution & sensor depth~30%

12 hardware parts, each tied to one workflow step. Full demo runs in Airplane Mode on the phone alone.

On-device AI integration~25%

Five local models on the Hexagon NPU; the SLM phrases SQL facts, so no hallucinated rupees.

Office Kit cross-device~15%

Customer-facing bill mirror + private cashier HUD, 1-click Excel sync at closing.
